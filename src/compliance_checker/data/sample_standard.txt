TIA-942 Data Center Infrastructure Standard — Spatial, Topological & Energy Requirements
=========================================================================================
(Expressed against the ArchDraft x FlowDraft unified-graph vocabulary:
 space categories = data_hall / electrical_room / plant_room / office / corridor;
 equipment node types = crac / crah / ups / pdu / chiller / rack.)

5.1 Aisles (Hot/Cold Aisle Containment)
Cold aisles shall be at least 1.2 m wide to permit front-of-rack access and
airflow. Hot aisles shall be at least 0.9 m wide for rear service access.

5.2 Data Hall (Computer Room)
Each data_hall shall have a usable area of at least 20 m2.

5.3 Electrical Room
At least one electrical_room must exist to house power distribution equipment.

5.4 Cooling Equipment
At least one CRAC (computer room air conditioner) unit must exist to provide
cooling to the data hall.

5.5 Uninterruptible Power Supply
At least one UPS must exist to provide ride-through power.

5.6 Power Topology
Every data_hall must connect to an electrical_room through the electrical
distribution topology (power cabling / busway).

5.7 Energy Efficiency
The facility Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) shall not exceed 1.5.
